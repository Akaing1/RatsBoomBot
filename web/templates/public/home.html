Everything in one place

Tools viewers enjoy and streamers can actually use.

Every channel profile can have its own commands, messages, currency, redeems, and enabled features. Select a panel to explore its command catalog.

01

Live chat and automation

Combined Twitch and YouTube chat, dashboard replies, OBS views, timers, and community messages tailored to each channel.

Available commands

!hi [username]!choice <options>!kaboom [username] !stinky [username]!lucky [username]!smart [username] !height [username]!pp [username]!lurk!help !stats [username]!socials!socials discord!socials youtube !set game <game name>!set title <stream title>!set discord <url>!set youtube <url>!timers [on|off]
02

Loyalty and achievements

Channel-specific currencies, games, daily check-ins, chatter levels, achievements, public profiles, and redeem history.

Currency command

!currency [username]!currency leaderboard !currency gamble <amount|all>!currency roulette <red|black|green> <amount> !currency duel <username> <amount>!currency give <username> <amount> !currency duel accept!currency duel decline !currency add <username> <amount>!currency reset

“Currency” is replaced by the channel's configured command, such as !shards or !mews. Check-ins and other redeems are activated through Twitch channel points.

03

Viewer activities

Viewer queues, clips, raid tools, community leaderboards, and interactions designed around the stream.

Available commands

!open!close!join!leave!queue !next!swap <position> <position>!requeue <position> <new position> !remove <position>!clear !clip!clip short!startraid <channel>
04

Game integrations

Profile-aware League and Overwatch commands for builds, ranked statistics, registration, and community ladders.

League of Legends

!champs!champs <champion>!register <Riot ID> [region] !unregister!rank [chatter]!ladder !build <champion>!runes <champion>!duo !custom lobby!custom lobby balance

Overwatch

!ow!owrank!owrecord <win|loss>!owreset
05

RPG raids

Join stream-wide boss fights, build stronger weapons, and earn raid rewards through contribution.

Raid commands

!raid!raid attack!raid shop!raid buy <item> !raid craft <sword|bow|tome>!raid equip <weapon>!raid unequip !raid sell <weapon>!raid repair <weapon>!raid loot !raid inventory!raid leaderboard!raid help
06

Responsible moderation

Protected users, permission-aware commands, cooldowns, shoutouts, and detailed per-stream event logs.

Available commands

!so <username>!kamikaze <username>!set slowmode <on|off>

Permission-sensitive actions are restricted to moderators or the broadcaster when appropriate.

The catalog shows what RatsBoomBot supports. Each channel receives its own configured subset, currency name, and channel-specific commands.

Simple streamer onboarding

Connect, customize, and go live.

Core onboarding is self-service. Specialized integrations and premium features may still require help from the developer through Discord.

1

Connect with Twitch

Authorize the channel you want to manage. RatsBoomBot never handles your Twitch password.

2

Make the dashboard yours

Choose features and configure your messages, currency, social links, commands, and permissions from the dashboard.

3

Connect and go live

Add optional YouTube or custom bot integrations, open your dashboard or OBS dock, and start streaming.
