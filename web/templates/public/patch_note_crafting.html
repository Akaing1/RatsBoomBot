Rats RPG patch notes

Crafting & Consumables

Weapon progression and new ways to prepare for raids.

Crafting introduced

Basic weapons now lead into refined and masterwork upgrades. Combine two matching weapons plus points: Basic + Basic + 5,000 points creates Refined (+80 damage); Refined + Refined + 25,000 points creates Masterwork (+150 damage).

Damaged weapons can still be crafted and the two matching weapons are consumed automatically. Use !raid craft refined <weapon> or !raid craft masterwork <weapon>.

More items in the raid shop

Power Potion now costs 1,500 points. Second Wind grants one additional attack, Berserk grants a one-time 5× attack with heavy recoil, and Blessing of the Gods gives all later stream attacks a 25% boost.

Mythical weapons remain non-craftable raid drops and now add +225 damage.

Use !raid help to view the complete shop and raid guide.
