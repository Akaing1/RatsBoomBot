Weapon crafting

Combine two copies of a tier to create the next one. Mythical drops remain separate.

⌄

Refined: two Basic weapons plus {{ "{:,}".format(config.refined_crafting_cost) }} points. Refined weapons add +{{ config.refined_weapon_attack }} damage.

Masterwork: two Refined weapons plus {{ "{:,}".format(config.masterwork_crafting_cost) }} points. Masterwork weapons add +{{ config.masterwork_weapon_attack }} damage.

Sword: {{ config.weapon_names.basic_sword }} → {{ config.weapon_names.refined_sword }} → {{ config.weapon_names.masterwork_sword }}. Mythical: {{ config.weapon_names.mythical_blade }}.

Points and drops

Raid rewards

Rewards scale with contribution and the outcome of the encounter.

⌄

Defeating a main boss distributes up to {{ "{:,}".format(config.reward_pool) }} points based on damage contribution, with a {{ "{:,}".format(config.final_hit_reward) }}-point final-hit bonus.

Mini bosses use a {{ "{:,}".format(config.mini_reward_pool) }}-point pool and a {{ "{:,}".format(config.mini_final_hit_reward) }}-point final-hit bonus.

Successful non-tutorial raids can drop mythical melee, ranged, or magic weapons. Tutorial participants receive a starter weapon they do not own, or {{ "{:,}".format(config.tutorial_complete_collection_points) }} points after completing the full starter collection.

Failed encounters still distribute a reduced portion of their reward pool according to contribution.
