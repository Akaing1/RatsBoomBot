Points and dropsRaid rewardsRewards use equal base shares, with bonuses for the highest-ranked contributors. ⌄

Defeating a boss starts with an equal base share of its full HP value for every contributor. The top 10% receive 2×, the next 10% receive 1.5×, the next 30% receive 1.25×, and everyone else receives the base share.

The final hitter also receives a {{ "{:,}".format(config.final_hit_reward) }}-point bonus on main bosses or {{ "{:,}".format(config.mini_final_hit_reward) }} points on mini bosses.

Successful non-tutorial raids can drop mythical melee, ranged, or magic weapons. Tutorial participants receive a starter weapon they do not own, or {{ "{:,}".format(config.tutorial_complete_collection_points) }} points after completing the full starter collection.

Failed encounters still distribute a reduced portion of their reward pool according to contribution.
